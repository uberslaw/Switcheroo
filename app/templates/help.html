Using Switcheroo

Short version for Client Services and Networks. Full setup notes live in the repo README.

Finding a port

Open Switches, pick a stack member, then click a port on the faceplate to fill the detail pane. Up/down is polled every {{ interval }}s; VLAN, MAC, IP, and ISE refresh daily or when you ask for a detail refresh (there is a {{ cooldown }}s cooldown per switch so we do not hammer a box).

Requesting a change

From the port pane you can request a VLAN change, a bounce, or a no-shutdown. VLAN changes need a reason — it goes on the ticket. Nothing is written to a switch until Networks approves it, unless an auto-approve policy matches. Track your own requests on Requests.

Something is broken — collect a diagnostic

If a poll, a switch write, or a ticket fails and the reason is not obvious, turn on step tracing before you retry:

  1. Open Launch Control on the Switcheroo host (scripts\Switcheroo-LaunchControl.cmd).
  2. Click Diagnostics ON. No restart is needed — it takes effect within one poll cycle.
  3. Reproduce the failure in the website.
  4. Click Open diagnostics.log and send the last lines, or attach {{ data_dir }}\diagnostics.log.

Each traced hop logs begin, then ok or fail with elapsed milliseconds, so the log shows which hop died (for example cisco.restconf fail ... error=ConnectTimeout) rather than just a stack trace. Passwords and webhook URLs are redacted. Turn Diagnostics OFF when you are done — DEBUG logging is noisy.
